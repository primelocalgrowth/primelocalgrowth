08Questions

What owners ask before they start.

Something else? Email adam@primelocalgrowth.com with your question.

How fast will I see results?
Listing improvements and review growth start showing within the first 30 days. Movement in the map results typically lands in 60 to 90 days depending on your competition. Every month ends with a written report. If the numbers aren't moving, you'll see that too, and we'll tell you why.
What does this cost?
There are three monthly packages: Google; Google + Social; and Full Growth, which adds a website upgrade and analytics. Every package is month to month, with no setup fee and no contract. Your free audit shows which package fits, and you get the price in writing before any work starts.
What if it doesn't work?
Then you stop, with no contract to buy out and everything still in your name. Nobody honest can guarantee rankings, because Google decides them. What you get instead is a written plan, a monthly report from your own Google dashboard and a results review at day 30, so you always know where you stand.
Can I try it free first?
The audit is free and yours to keep, with the three fixes that would help you fastest. The work itself is paid month to month from the start, and you can stop any month.
Who owns my Google profile and logins?
You do. Your profile, logins, photos and posts stay in your name. We are added as a manager, and you can remove that access from your own account at any time.
Where will my business show up?
Everywhere customers search before they call: Google Search and Maps, Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp, Nextdoor and social media. Your name, hours and phone number are corrected so they match on every one, and the higher packages add social posting and a website upgrade.
What do the monthly reports show?
Calls, direction requests, website clicks and new reviews, pulled from your own Google dashboard, plus the work done that month. Weak months are reported the same way as strong ones.
Do you buy fake reviews?
Never. Fake reviews get listings suspended. We build simple systems that make it easy for your real, happy customers to say what they already think. That's why the results hold up.
