3 fixes that would help fastest

1. Get the Google Business Profile claimed, verified and visible

Without a visible profile, every nearby search for a barber went to someone else, however good the cuts were.

What to do: Recover or recreate the profile, verify it, then add services, hours and the booking link.

2. Close the review gap

The shops customers saw had between 21 and 219 reviews, and the middle one had 54. A shop with no visible reviews gives a stranger nothing to trust.

What to do: Send real customers a one-tap review link after each visit, and aim for the first 25 in about 60 days.

3. Make the name, address and phone match everywhere

The shop's details were spread across Instagram, its website and its booking page, with small differences. Google trusts details that agree.

What to do: Pick one version of the name, address and phone, then update every place it appears.
